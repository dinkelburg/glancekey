Download GlanceKey

You need the iPhone app, and GlanceKey on each computer you want to unlock. Then pair them by scanning a QR code; the docs walk you through it.

iPhone

Approve with Face ID, from anywhere. Needs iOS 17 or later.

Coming soonto the App Store

GlanceKey for iPhone is in testing now.

Mac

The menu bar app. Signed and checked by Apple, for macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

Download for Mac

Or with Homebrew:

brew install --cask dinkelburg/glancekey/glancekey

Linux

One command installs it and starts it with your session (systemd). For Omarchy, hyprlock, GNOME and KDE.

curl -fsSL https://www.glancekey.com/install.sh | sh

Or download the program for x86-64 or ARM64 (checksums), or use Homebrew:

brew install dinkelburg/glancekey/glancekey-agent
